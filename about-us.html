About Us

Leather handbags and accessories, made in Kolkata since 2007.

Our Pride

Experience and Expertise Since 2007

Sima International has been a leading manufacturer and exporter of premium leather handbags and accessories since 2007. Based in Kolkata, we are committed to delivering exceptional craftsmanship to markets across Europe and Asia.

Our in-house tannery lets us maintain strict quality control and use only the finest raw materials, so every bag we ship reflects the care that went into it.
