About Sima International

Trusted Partner for Premium & Bulk Leather Goods

Sima International has been a leading manufacturer and exporter of premium leather handbags and accessories since 2007. Based in Kolkata, our in-house tannery lets us maintain strict quality control and use only the finest raw materials.

We are committed to delivering exceptional craftsmanship to markets across Europe and Asia.

Manufacturing in Kolkata

Kolkata Leather Manufacturing Excellence

Sima International runs its own tannery, workshop and office in Kolkata. Skilled craftspeople turn carefully selected leather into handbags and accessories, with quality checked at every stage.

Being a manufacturer with an in-house tannery lets us offer:

  • Direct control over leather quality
  • Skilled, careful craftsmanship
  • Strict quality checks before shipping
  • Export experience across Europe and Asia

That is why buyers choose to work with Sima directly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about Sima International, our products and how we work.

Is Sima International a manufacturer or a trading company?

Sima International is a manufacturer and exporter of premium leather handbags and accessories. We run our own tannery and workshop in Kolkata.

Where are your facilities located?

All our sites are in Kolkata, India: our office on Rafi Ahmed Kidwai Road, our workshop on E T Road, and our tannery at Kolkata Leather Complex.

What products do you make?

We make premium leather handbags for women, a men’s collection of leather bags, and accessories such as wallets and small leather goods.

Do you have your own tannery?

Yes. Our in-house tannery lets us control leather quality before production begins and use only the finest raw materials.

Which markets do you export to?

We supply buyers across Europe and Asia.

How do you ensure quality?

We apply strict quality control at every stage, from tanning and cutting to stitching, finishing and final inspection.

How long have you been in business?

Sima International has been making and exporting leather goods since 2007.

How can I contact you for bulk or special orders?

Use the enquiry form on this page, call +91 98308 33173, or email sima_international@yahoo.com. Tell us your styles, quantities and target market and we will get back to you.
